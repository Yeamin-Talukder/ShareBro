Share tab · Apple-style Orbit

Orbit, rebuilt the way an Apple system app would do it (think AirDrop crossed with Settings). Nearby devices sit around you on the orbit; everything else is quiet grouped lists, real switches and menus. Shown in a green theme. Everything in the phone is clickable.

What's Apple about it

  • AirDrop-style sending. Tap a device: what you picked flies into it, a ring fills around it and the label goes Waiting… → Sending 40% → Sent, right where you tapped.
  • Touch and hold a device for a context menu (Send, Add to Favourites, Copy Address) instead of a hidden long-press.
  • The ⋯ menu next to "Nearby" holds Scan Again, Find Phones Without Wi‑Fi and Connect by Address.
  • Grouped lists, real switches, title-style labels. Options are a Settings-style list with coloured icon tiles; Link or QR Code and Other Apps are rows with chevrons.
  • Colour is used sparingly. Green marks only the primary action, live status and switches; everything else is monochrome on a deep green aurora, and glass is used only for the floating buttons and the dock.
  • Receive is the same orbit with you in the middle: it lights up when you start receiving, and whoever sends to you appears on it with a progress ring.
