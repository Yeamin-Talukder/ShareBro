Trip group · demo

Everyone in the group has ShareBro. Each phone learns its owner's face from a selfie, everyone picks the photos they're happy to share, and each photo goes only to the people who are in it. Photos with nobody from the group in them aren't sent.

How it works

  • Group: one phone shows a QR code; the others scan it with ShareBro. Members pop in as they join and get a tick once their face is set up.
  • This is me: three quick angles fill a ring of ticks, like setting up face unlock. Only a face fingerprint (numbers, not the selfie) is shared, and only inside this group.
  • Pick photos: nothing is shared unless you select it. Small dots show who the app thinks is in each photo; day chips help pick fast.
  • Who's in it: your phone checks each photo against the group's faces. A copy flies to each person in it; photos with nobody from the group stay with you.
  • Quick check: faces the app isn't sure about (sunglasses, turned away) are asked once, Yes or No, so nothing goes to the wrong person.
  • Sending: every friend's phone gets only their photos, accepted automatically because they're in the group. A friend out of range gets theirs when they're back.
  • Your photos: on your side, the photos friends took of you arrive in one album, sorted by who sent them.
