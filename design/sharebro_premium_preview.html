ShareBro, the premium edition

Quiet luxury: deep ink or warm ivory, champagne-gold hairlines, serif titles with light sans text, more breathing room and fewer, calmer animations.

Palette

One accent only, champagne gold, used for lines, the active tab and the main button. Everything else is ink, ivory or muted taupe.

Typography

Cormorant Garamond (serif) for titles, names and numbers; Inter Light for body text; small spaced capitals for labels like "Sending" or "214 photos".

Shapes & lines

Thin 1 px gold hairlines instead of coloured fills, monogram avatars, round outlined icons, generous padding, and a frosted dock with a single gold dot under the active tab.

Motion

Slower, softer and fewer: items rise in gently, the transfer arc uses fine dotted gold, and celebrations become a subtle shimmer instead of confetti.
