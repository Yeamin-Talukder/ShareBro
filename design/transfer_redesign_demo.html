Sending & receiving · new style

The live transfer screens and the Sending list redone in the flat look. The two-phones animation stays (it's good); everything around it now matches the rest of the app.

What's new

  • Same stage, new look: both phones are flat circles (you in the accent, them in their device colour), joined by a marching dashed arc. Photos fly across it and their phone bumps as each one lands.
  • Clear stage line: one line under the phones says what's happening: looking for the phone, waiting for them to accept, sending, done, or why it failed.
  • Finished: their phone turns accent with a tick, the arc goes solid and flat dots burst out (accent, not green).
  • Numbers: a big rolling percent, a flat progress bar, then a stats card (speed, time left, files) like the scan screen.
  • Files card: every photo or file as a tile that lights up with an accent tick as it arrives.
  • Sending list: big title, a "Send to everyone" card with one accent button, Receive as a Settings-style switch row, and live transfers as rows with progress rings. When nothing's sending, a small flat animation of a paper plane flying between two phones.
