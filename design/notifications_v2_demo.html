Notifications: before and after

Each card shows today's notification and the proposed one, as they'd look in Android's shade. Names and pictures are made up.

Also changes behind the scenes

  • Tapping opens the right screen: a scan opens Scan, a request opens Receive, a finished send opens its transfer, received photos open the album. Today every tap lands on the People page.
  • Finished transfers stack into one ShareBro group with a summary (“3 transfers done”) instead of a pile of separate notices.
  • The extra “ShareBro is working” notice stays out of sight: still needed by Android to keep working in the background, but silent and folded at the bottom of the shade.
  • One tone of voice: titles say what happened, the line under says what to do; no ✓ marks or emoji in titles.
  • Quiet when you're in the app: the in-app island shows it; the shade only gets what you'd miss.
