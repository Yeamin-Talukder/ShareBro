Scan screen · new style

The scanning screen redone in the flat look: no shaded radar, no old chips. A scan line passes over each photo, viewfinder corners snap around every face it finds, and the faces fly down onto a conveyor of everyone found so far.

What's new

  • Scan line, not a radar: a thin accent line sweeps down each photo and leaves a faint tint behind it, like a document scanner.
  • Viewfinder corners: each face gets four corners that snap in like a camera's face focus, with a small "face" tag.
  • Face conveyor: faces fly from the photo into a row that slides along to make room, with the total next to it.
  • Stats card: faces, people so far and time left as Settings-style tiles; a number bumps when it goes up.
  • Milestones drop in as a black pill with flat sparkles, like the island.
  • Finished: a solid accent check orb spins in, flat confetti bursts, and everyone's faces fly out of the orb into place.
