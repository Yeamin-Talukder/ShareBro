Whole app · Share style

Every tab with the same look as the new Share page: big bold titles, grouped rounded cards, Settings-style rows with coloured icon tiles, capsule switches and chips, and Material icons. Colours are flat and simple: one accent colour, no gradients. Tap the dock to switch tabs; open a person or Settings to see inner pages.

Try it

  • People: faces sit in rings (named people in the accent, new faces dashed with "Add a name"). Trips and groups are capsule chips. Drag one face onto another to merge.
  • Photos: bold day headers, a tight grid, and Select brings a glass action bar (Send, Share, Favourite, Album, Trash) in place of the dock.
  • Albums: Favourites, Videos and Trash as simple cards with a coloured icon, then albums as rounded mosaic covers.
  • Explore: a duplicates row in a card, then swipeable category cards with the name under the cover.
  • Settings: the strictness slider shows "≈ 32 people" in the accent colour; everything else is grouped rows with switches.
  • A person: a big face ring, a Send button, and an All / Solo / Group switch above their photos.
