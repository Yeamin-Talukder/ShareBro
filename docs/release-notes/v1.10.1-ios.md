**ShareBro for iPhone 1.10.1** is the first iPhone and iPad version. It's the same app as on Android: people grouped by face on the phone, trips, search, and sending each friend only the photos they're in, over Wi-Fi or a hotspot.

This is an **early preview**: it was built and checked on a Mac but **hasn't been tested on an iPhone yet**, so expect rough edges and please [report anything odd](https://github.com/Yeamin-Talukder/ShareBro/issues).

## Download

| File | For |
|---|---|
| **`ShareBro-1.10.1-ios-unsigned.ipa`** | iPhone and iPad with iOS 15.5 or newer |

The app isn't on the App Store and isn't signed by Apple yet, so you can't tap the file to install it. You install it with a free sideloading tool that signs it with your own Apple ID.

## Install

1. On your computer, install [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io) (Windows or Mac). [AltStore](https://altstore.io) works too.
2. Connect your iPhone with a cable, unlock it, and tap **Trust** if it asks.
3. Drag **`ShareBro-1.10.1-ios-unsigned.ipa`** into Sideloadly, enter your Apple ID, and click **Start**.
4. On the iPhone, open **Settings › General › VPN & Device Management**, tap your Apple ID and tap **Trust**.
5. On iOS 16 or newer, also turn on **Settings › Privacy & Security › Developer Mode** (the phone restarts).
6. Open ShareBro. Allow **Photos** so it can find faces, and **Local Network** so it can find your friends' phones and computers.

**With a free Apple ID the app stops opening after 7 days.** Connect the phone and install it again with Sideloadly to renew it: your people and settings are kept.

## What's different on iPhone

- **Received files** that aren't photos or videos go to the **Files** app, under **On My iPhone › ShareBro**. Photos and videos go into your photo library.
- **Scanning pauses** when you leave the app: iPhones don't let apps keep scanning in the background like Android does.
- **Finding devices** works by checking your Wi-Fi network, so it can take a few seconds longer than on Android.
- **No in-app updates.** Download each new version here and install it the same way.
- Sending with no network at all (Bluetooth / Wi-Fi Direct) and sending installed apps are **Android only**.
