**ShareBro for Mac 1.10.0** is the first Mac version: scan folders of photos for faces, group them into people, and send and receive with your phone and friends on the same Wi-Fi. Everything stays on your Mac.

This is a **preview**: please [report anything odd](https://github.com/Yeamin-Talukder/ShareBro/issues).

## Download

| File | For |
|---|---|
| **`ShareBro-1.10.0-macos.dmg`** | Installing: macOS 12 Monterey or newer, Apple Silicon and Intel Macs |
| `ShareBro-1.10.0-macos.zip` | The same app, zipped. The app's own updater uses this one |

1. Download the **.dmg** and open it. In the window that appears, drag **ShareBro** onto **Applications**, then eject the disk.
2. The app isn't signed by Apple yet, so the first time macOS says it **can't check it for malicious software**. Click **Done**, then open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** next to ShareBro. You only do this once.
3. If macOS asks whether ShareBro may accept incoming network connections, click **Allow**, so your phone can find the Mac.

## What it does

- **Scan any folder** (Downloads, an external drive, a network share) and group the faces into people, with the same face model as the phone and Windows versions, so people match across your devices.
- **Faster on Apple's chips:** face finding runs on Core ML (the Mac's graphics chip and Neural Engine), about 11% faster than the CPU alone on an Apple Silicon MacBook.
- **Send and receive** with ShareBro on phones and PCs, the LocalSend apps and any web browser (QR share). Choose where received files go in **Settings › Sharing**.
- **Help your phone scan:** the Mac finds faces for the phone over Wi-Fi.
- **Sync people** with your phone and other computers (names and face fingerprints, never photos).
- **Updates itself** from inside the app (Settings › Software update) once newer Mac releases come out.

Not yet on the Mac: scene labels, smile and open-eye scores (phones only). Get the phone app from [ShareBro 1.10.0](https://github.com/Yeamin-Talukder/ShareBro/releases/tag/v1.10.0).
